# Scan Man Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

Scan Man is a document scanner that works entirely on your device.

**What we collect**: nothing. Scan Man has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network connections.

**Your documents**: scans, recognized text and notes are stored only on your device, encrypted by iOS while the device is locked. Text recognition runs on the device. Documents leave your device only when you choose to share or export them, for example through the share sheet, AirDrop or the Files app.

**Camera**: used only to scan documents while you are scanning.

**Face ID / Touch ID**: used only to unlock the app when you turn on app lock. Biometric data is handled by iOS and is never available to Scan Man.

**Photos and files**: Scan Man only reads the photos and files you pick, share to it, or place in its import folder.

**Deleting data**: deleting a scan removes it from the device. Deleting the app removes all scans.

**Contact**: open an issue at https://github.com/tissanr/Scan-Man.
